# CLAUDE.md

## Design work

For any UI or design work in this repo (building, redesigning, restyling, polishing or reviewing pages and components), use the `taste-skill:design-taste-frontend` skill.

### Design direction (the owner's choice, wins over the skill where they conflict)

The look follows https://10years.pixelmatters.com/: navy and cream themes (dark is the main look, light follows the
system by default), glass containers, soft colour orbs, pieces that sit a little tilted and offset, and pictures that
fade into the ground with a glow. Image slots stay grey boxes until the owner adds photos: the portrait is square (or
a circle), every other picture is landscape. Where the skill's rules say otherwise (no tilt, equal edges, one section
per screen, dark pages only for tech, stand-in photographs), keep this direction.
